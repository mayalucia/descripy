* Why this document exists

descripy is a tool for recording /how a value came to be believed/. So
before we restructure its code we state its theory of knowledge — and
then we let that theory cut the code into modules and fix the public API.
The tool's first act of provenance is on itself: descripy's own
epistemology becomes the lens we rebuild descripy through.

This document is therefore /both/ discursive (a theory we reason through)
and generative (its last section is the module layout and the public API,
derived — not decorated — from the theory). It is downstream of the
design contract
(=domains/bravli/codev/principles/descripy-the-descriptor-as-seed.org=)
and of the first build-by-use spike (=examples/=, =examples/LEARNINGS.org=).

* Part A — The epistemology of the tool

** The shift: beliefs about values, not values

Industrial provenance records =dt = 0.025=. descripy records =dt = 0.025=
/and how that came to be believed/ — who proposed it, who dissented, what
was decided, what stays open. The unit of knowledge is not the value but
the /belief about the value/. (Manifesto, "Provenance of belief, not
merely provenance of value.")

** The four standings a value can hold

A value carries its /epistemic status/ — its honesty about itself
(manifesto §2):

- *known* — established; e.g. a measured or reference-fixed quantity.
- *inferred* — derived from other knowns by a stated step.
- *speculated* — a guess we should sweep / revisit.
- *borrowed-from-reference* — taken from an external source we are
  reproducing (e.g. =dt=0.025= from the NEURON reference, =diam= from
  Hay 2011).

This is "separate known from inferred from speculated" made /structural/,
not advisory. The config does not pretend every number is equally solid.

** Two axes of knowledge descripy keeps distinct

The spike surfaced a second, orthogonal distinction (LEARNINGS.org), which
the four standings above do not capture and must not be conflated with:

- *intent vs consequence.* /Intent/ is what a mind chose (=L=10=,
  =dt=0.025=, =pas.g=0.0001=). /Consequence/ is what the world then
  computed (=Ra=35.4=, the voltage trace, the =psection()= receipt). The
  lean seed records intent; germination /re-derives/ consequence; the two
  meeting again is the verification.

So a value sits at the crossing of two axes:
  - /standing/  : known | inferred | speculated | borrowed   (how sure)
  - /role/      : intent | consequence                        (chosen vs computed)

=dt=0.025= is *borrowed* (standing) and *intent* (role). =Ra=35.4= is
*known* (a NEURON default) and *consequence* (role). Keeping these axes
separate is the heart of descripy's epistemology.

** The seed, not the archive

A record is kept not for audit but as /material for regeneration/
(manifesto, "The record as a seed, not an archive"). The test of "did we
record enough?" is the *legibility / cold-regeneration* test: a mind who
was not present, given only the serialized bytes, can regenerate the gist.
Anything that does not help that regeneration is noise.

This makes regeneration descripy's criterion of /truth-carrying/: a seed
is adequate iff its consequence can be re-derived from its recorded
intent and a recorded environment. (The spike's environment-relativity
finding: the oracle is only valid against the environment that produced
it.)

** Mechanism, not policy — the boundary of what descripy may know

descripy /records, serializes, computes/ over belief data. It /interprets
nothing, enforces nothing/ (manifesto, "Governing principle: mechanism,
not policy"). Gates, guides, approvals, regeneration-as-workflow are
/policy/ and live in a reference package, not the substrate. The honest
answer to "what can this library do to me?" is "nothing — it only
computes and returns."

Epistemically: descripy may know /that/ a value is speculated; it may not
decide /what to do/ about a speculated value. The first is a fact it
records; the second is a judgement it must refuse.

* Part B — The epistemology applied to descripy's own code

We now turn descripy's four standings onto the substrate itself. For each
piece of =descripy/= we ask: do we *know* its contract (it is exercised,
its behavior is clear), do we *infer* it (it looks load-bearing but is
untested / undocumented), or are we *speculating* (pasted, opaque, maybe
vestigial)?

The spike already drew the first boundary by /use/: the entire
prototype — soma, seed, germinate, belief, 12 tests — imports exactly
three names: =parameter=, =lazyfield=, =Config=, which transitively need
=field=, =Struct=, =section=, =serialize_value=, =NA=.

*The rule this map yields:* characterization tests are urgent exactly for
the /inferred/ rows (pin them before any edit), free to cut for the
/speculated/ rows (history preserves them), and safe to leave for the
/known/ rows (the spike already pins them).

/Update (2026-06-30):/ the four /inferred/ rows (=field=, =lazyfield=,
=Struct=, =serialize_value=) are now pinned by =tests/test_substrate.py=
(18 characterization tests) and promoted to /known/. Pinning them also
surfaced two /dead branches/ — =field.__get__='s line-90 =ValueError=
fallthrough and =field.__set__delegated= — both unreachable because the
required-field invariant is enforced in the constructor, not the getter.
The carve may delete them. With the trunk now /known/, the urgent-test
gate is cleared and Part C's restructure may proceed.

This is descripy's thesis used as method: we are recording what we /know/
vs /infer/ vs /speculate/ about descripy's own parts, and letting that
standing decide the action — exactly what the tool asks its users to do
with their values.

* Part C — The generative cut (module layout + public API)

The two axes (standing × role) and the mechanism/policy boundary force a
shape. The substrate holds only what is /mechanism/; standing and role
are /data the substrate carries/, never logic it acts on.

** The belief layer is core, and exported (resolved)

- voice: mu2tau (human)
- decision: =belief.py= ships /in/ descripy and is exported by default.

The mechanism/policy boundary, applied reflexively: withholding belief to
stay "neutral" would itself be a policy ("you may not record dissent
unless you opt in at import"). So the substrate /offers/ the belief
mechanism — =Believed=, the attributed-turn schema, serialization of
turns — and stays silent on whether you must use it. *The opt-in happens
when the programmer uses it*, not at import. A config with no turns is not
descripy refusing belief; it is a user who recorded none. What stays out
of the substrate is only the /elicitation/ workflow (prompting for
dissent, gating on open questions) — that is policy, and lives in a
reference package.

** Module layout (derived from Part A/B + the decision above)

: descripy/
:   field.py      # the descriptor: field, lazyfield, field_of_type   (was struct.py, part 1)
:   struct.py     # the container: Struct, section, serialize_value    (was struct.py, part 2)
:   config.py     # Config, parameter — the user-facing config object
:   serialize.py  # the receipt normalizer (from the spike); serialize_value to move here
:   status.py     # the four standings (known/inferred/speculated/borrowed) as data
:   belief.py     # Believed + the attributed-turn schema — CORE, exported
:   nothing.py    # NA  (promoted out of utils — it is core, not a util)
:   _attic/       # entity.py, record.struct(), serialize_doesnt_work_for_nested — speculated, parked

/Carve note (2026-06-30, step 1 — struct.py split done):/ =field.py= and
=struct.py= are separated; all 30 tests green. The =field -> Struct= cycle
(=field.validate= type-guards on =Struct=) is broken with a function-local
import inside =validate=, so =field.py= carries no module-level dependency
on the container — the descriptor stays the dependency leaf.
=serialize_value= /stays in struct.py/ for now (it dispatches on =Struct=,
so moving it to =serialize.py= would re-introduce the cycle there);
=serialize.py= will absorb it once it also hosts the receipt normalizer
that imports both. Dependency, not topic, sets the boundary.

/Carve note (2026-06-30, step 2 — nothing.py promoted):/ =NA= moved from
=utils/nothing.py= to =descripy/nothing.py= — core, not util, because it
sits on the seed path and is exported. It still leans on
=utils.singleton.Singleton= (core may use a util; a util must not need
core). Three importers updated (=struct=, =config=, =test_substrate=);
=descripy.NA= still resolves; 30 tests green.

/Carve note (2026-06-30, step 3 — speculated pieces parked):/ =record.py=
and =entity.py= moved to =descripy/_attic/= (via =git mv=, lineage kept);
=Config.serialize_doesnt_work_for_nested= deleted (broken-by-name, on no

NA, serialize_value= (+ the error types). The attic is reachable only by
explicit =descripy._attic.*= import. 30 tests green.

** Public API (what `import descripy` should expose)

Driven by use (the spike needed three) plus the named surfaces the
manifesto commits to, plus the belief layer (core, per the decision). The
public surface:

: from descripy import (
:     Config, parameter, field, lazyfield, section,   # describe
:     Struct, NA,                                      # substrate primitives
:     serialize_value,                                 # serialize
:     Believed,                                        # belief — offered, never required
:     # status: the four standings as values, once Part A §"standings" lands
: )

Everything in =_attic/= is /not/ exported. The public surface is exactly
what is *known* or deliberately *promoted from inferred-with-tests* —
never what is *speculated*. =Believed= is exported as /mechanism/: present
in the surface, used at the programmer's discretion.
